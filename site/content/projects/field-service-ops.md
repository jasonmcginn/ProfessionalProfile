## What this is

One app for a field technician's day: truck stock, cycle counts, tickets and shipping. I built it for my own field work, to remove the overhead of filling out forms and templates.

## What it does

<div class="cards2 c3">
<div>
<p class="k">Tickets from email</p>
<p>It reads a dedicated notifications folder in my inbox. Pattern rules identify each notification, and the app creates or updates the ticket.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="k">Stock, counts and shipping</p>
<p>Truck stock, cycle counts and shipping sit in the same app as the tickets.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="k">A parts kit kept current</p>
<p>A built-in product database has a service manual indexer. It extracts part details and updates the kit items.</p>
</div>
</div>

## How it runs

It runs in Docker with encrypted data, and it has a phone app. It is documented with a decisions log and a walk-through guide.
