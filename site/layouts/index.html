// 01 how I work

One approach, applied to very different problems.

Twenty-plus years across networking, systems, cloud and automation. Three principles run through all of it: I build secure by default, work methodically from diagnosis to verification, and go deep enough to know a system cold.

security by default

Systems that work without being exposed.

An app demanded local admin; the students couldn't have it. I monitored exactly what it needed and granted only that by Group Policy, nothing more exposed than it had to be.

full story →

methodical delivery

Diagnose, document, propose, verify.

A teacher logging a class of twenty-one five-year-olds in by hand, every session. I worked it end to end and wrote it up: policy first, then identity, devices, and the people, a proposal they could act on.

full story →

depth over guesswork

Neglected systems, made trustworthy.

A switching loop took the network down for four hours. I found it cable by cable, then rebuilt and documented it so any fault traces from the wall jack to the switch port. Untangling it is how I came to know it cold.

// 05 beyond the keyboard

A maker, on and off the clock.

I'm a maker at heart. I like building things that have a real use and purpose, with a design that is both elegant and functional. When I'm away from the keyboard I'm usually in the woods, or out in the shop making something with my hands.

A lot of that happens at the CNC, where I enjoy the design side as much as the carving. I designed my own cribbage board and have sold dozens of them. I also turn and carve bowls, pens, and plenty of other woodworking projects, tons of them over the years. And I love dogs.

It's the same instinct that shapes how I build systems. I want what I make to last, and to actually get used.
